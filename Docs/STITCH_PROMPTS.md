# Oloz — Stitch Screen Prompts

Use with DESIGN.md attached. Paste the Global Prompt first, then one screen prompt at a time.

## Global Prompt (paste first)
Design "Oloz", a screen time and reel-scroll tracking app for mobile and desktop. Follow the attached DESIGN.md exactly: warm off-white background (#F6F5F2), white cards with 20px radius, soft indigo primary (#6B83D6), peach accent for reels (#F0A98E), Inter font, outline icons, very little text, lots of whitespace, flat soft shadows. Clean, high-end, Figma-quality, professional, eye-soft.

### 1. Home

Mobile App Detail screen for Instagram. Back arrow, app icon and name. Large "1h 48m" today. 7-day bar chart. Two small cards: "Reels 142" and "Opens 23". One button at the bottom: "Set limit". Clean and minimal.

### 5. Set Limit (bottom sheet)
Mobile bottom sheet over App Detail. Title "Daily limit". Big time stepper showing 1h 30m with minus and plus buttons. Toggle "Remind me". Primary button "Save". Rounded top corners, soft overlay.

### 6. Settings
Mobile Settings screen. Short grouped list rows with label and value or toggle: Goals and limits, Notifications, Theme (Light / Dark / Auto), Permissions, Devices. Minimal icons, no descriptions. Bottom tab bar, Settings active.

### 7. Onboarding 1: Welcome
Mobile welcome screen. Centered simple Oloz logo mark, one-line tagline "Know your scroll.", primary button "Get started". Lots of whitespace, soft background.

### 8. Onboarding 2: Permission
Mobile permission screen. Simple icon in a soft circle, title "Allow usage access", one short line, primary button "Allow", text button "Not now". Step indicator with 3 dots.

### 9. Onboarding 3: Goal
Mobile goal screen. Title "Daily goal". Time stepper (3h) and reel goal stepper (100). Primary button "Done", text button "Skip".

### 10. Over-limit Alert
Mobile full-screen gentle alert. Soft danger-tinted icon, title "Limit reached", caption "Instagram, 1h 30m". Two buttons: "Take a break" (primary) and "5 more min" (secondary). Calm, non-shaming.
